Scenario explanation

DRYING

Drip stays OFF throughout. Starting at 28%, moisture gradually falls as the model retains 90% of the previous reading per step.

WETTING

Drip stays ON after the initial sample. Starting at 5%, moisture rises toward the model's 30% equilibrium.

STRESS CYCLE

Starting at 18%, irrigation switches ON once the previous model reading reaches 15% or below. It then stays ON; this rule has no automatic shutoff.

OPTIMAL

Starting at 22%, drip switches ON at or below 15%, OFF at or above 30%, and otherwise keeps its previous setting. This is threshold-based control.

SCHEDULED

Drip is ON except for samples 48–59 (hours 24–29.5), when it is OFF, regardless of moisture. Sample 0 starts OFF in every case.

Verdict is based on the final model value, not the noisy sensor: ≤15% = TURN ON (STRESS); ≥30% = SHUT OFF (FULL); otherwise MAINTAIN (OPTIMAL). The verdict reports a recommendation and does not override the selected scenario's control rule.

Drip control

The model follows y[k] = 0.9 × y[k−1] + 0.03 × u[k]. Sensor readings add Gaussian noise (σ = 0.005) and are rounded to 0.001.
